## Related: start-up flows of viscoelastic fluids

The companion repository
[**viscoelastic-startup**](https://github.com/ichristov/viscoelastic-startup) ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.22930189](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22930189)) verifies published "exact solutions" for the start-up of plane Couette flow and for Stokes' first and second problems for second-grade, Oldroyd-B (Jeffreys) and Gordon&ndash;Schowalter fluids, where a lost Dirac delta leaves the published formulas describing a plate ramped on the retardation time. The counterparts of [startup PC flow](startup_PC_flow.ipynb) are [christov_2013_oldroydb_couette](https://github.com/ichristov/viscoelastic-startup/blob/main/notebooks/christov_2013_oldroydb_couette.ipynb) for Oldroyd-B and [jordan_2005_second_grade_couette](https://github.com/ichristov/viscoelastic-startup/blob/main/notebooks/jordan_2005_second_grade_couette.ipynb) for the second-grade fluid.

Enjoy. Cheers! 🍺
